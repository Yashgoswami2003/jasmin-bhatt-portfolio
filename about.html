Jasmin Bhatt — Storyteller, Artist & Legal/Tax Mind.

Writer | Director | Actor | Poet | Author | Storyteller | Educator | Tax Consultant

Jasmin Bhatt is an Indian Writer, Director, Actor, Poet, Novelist, Storyteller, Educator, and Tax Consultant from Bhavnagar, Gujarat, India. His creative journey encompasses literature, poetry, theatre, filmmaking, storytelling, and digital entertainment.

He writes and develops original stories for Gujarati and Hindi feature films, web series, short films, and audio entertainment platforms. His literary and cinematic works explore human relationships, love, emotions, suspense, mystery, crime, psychological drama, and social realities.

Rooted in a passion for literature, poetry, theatre, and cinema, his interest in creative expression grew into a multifaceted career spanning novels, theatrical productions, poetry performances, short films, feature films, web series, and audio dramas. Working across Gujarati, Hindi, and English, he aims to bring regional stories and universal human experiences to a wider audience—while balancing an extensive background in taxation, law, and education.
